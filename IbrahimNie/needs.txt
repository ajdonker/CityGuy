Needs
1. He needs a way to find places that satisfy his specific situational needs.
For example, when choosing a place, he considers transportation time, cost, and whether the environment is quiet. His regular park use also shows that a place can serve a very specific purpose—in his case, feeding stray animals.    
2. He needs a way to obtain reliable and up-to-date information about places.
He described two concrete failures: an AI-like application gave him information about a place that did not exist, and map information was sometimes outdated, causing him to arrive at places that no longer existed.    
3. He needs a way to discover useful places that are difficult to find through mainstream online information sources.
He explained that information about less-famous or specialized shops can be difficult to find with his current tools.    
4. He needs a way to share useful information without being discouraged by hostile or judgmental responses from other users.
Solutions
1. Context-aware place discovery
A place-discovery system that helps users find locations according to their specific needs and context—for example, finding a park suitable for walking, running, relaxing, or other specific activities.
2. Trustworthy community information sharing
A community information-sharing system that helps users assess the reliability and freshness of information about places, for example through multiple user contributions, verification mechanisms, timestamps, or other indicators of confidence.
