Needs

1. He needs to find places that suit his activities and conditions at the time of his visit.
   Possible solutions: Filters for facilities and accessibility; comparable place summaries; recent crowding reports; questions answered by previous visitors; recommendations based on activities such as exercising, relaxing, or meeting friends.
2. He needs to judge whether information is current and trustworthy.
   Possible solutions: Show when information was observed; prioritize recent photos and reviews; let visitors confirm existing reports; flag information that needs updating; highlight recommendations from trusted contacts.
3. He needs to discover useful local places and knowledge missing from mainstream sources.
   Possible solutions: Citizen-submitted place listings; neighborhood recommendation collections; community maps of routes and facilities; local discovery walks; assistance for small businesses to publish basic information.
4. He needs to share useful firsthand information with little effort and confidence in its accuracy.
   Possible solutions: Short contribution templates; automatic location and observation-time fields; quick photo-based updates; drafts that can be completed later; simple options to update or withdraw information when conditions change.
5. He needs to warn others about hazards and know where to send reports.
   Possible solutions: A directory of responsible organizations; links to relevant official reporting forms; reports containing a location and photo; temporary community hazard alerts; easily shareable warnings for friends and nearby users.
