## Why Was He Chosen? Why Is He Appropriate?

He is a real estate agent living in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. His job requires him to explore different locations and understand local environments. He frequently uses Google Maps and social media to discover places but sometimes encounters missing or unreliable information. His experience provides valuable insights into local place discovery, information reliability, and public space issues. I consider him an experienced immediate user.

## How Was He Recruited?

Tuan Anh Nguyen Vu was recruited through personal contact, as he is a friend of the interviewer. He was invited to participate in an online interview for our HCI project. He agreed voluntarily to share his experiences of discovering, using, and sharing information about public spaces in Ho Chi Minh City.

## Where Was the Interview Conducted?

The interview was conducted via video call.

## What Did You Observe and Ask?

During the interview, we asked Tuan Anh about his habits of visiting public spaces, how he discovers new places, and which tools he uses to find information. We also explored the difficulties he faces when searching for reliable and up-to-date information, his trust in online reviews, and his willingness to share or report local issues. We observed that he relies heavily on Google Maps, social media, and friends' recommendations, but often struggles to find accurate information about current conditions, safety, cleanliness, and crowd levels.

## Needs and Possible Solutions

### 1. Finding Places That Match Activities and Conditions

He needs to find places that suit his activities and conditions at the time of his visit.

**Possible solutions:**

- Filters for facilities and accessibility.
- Comparable place summaries.
- Recent crowding reports.
- Questions answered by previous visitors.
- Recommendations based on activities such as exercising, relaxing, or meeting friends.

### 2. Judging Information Freshness and Trustworthiness

He needs to judge whether information is current and trustworthy.

**Possible solutions:**

- Show when information was observed.
- Prioritize recent photos and reviews.
- Let visitors confirm existing reports.
- Flag information that needs updating.
- Highlight recommendations from trusted contacts.

### 3. Discovering Local Places and Knowledge

He needs to discover useful local places and knowledge missing from mainstream sources.

**Possible solutions:**

- Citizen-submitted place listings.
- Neighborhood recommendation collections.
- Community maps of routes and facilities.
- Local discovery walks.
- Assistance for small businesses to publish basic information.

### 4. Sharing Firsthand Information Efficiently and Accurately

He needs to share useful firsthand information with little effort and confidence in its accuracy.

**Possible solutions:**

- Short contribution templates.
- Automatic location and observation-time fields.
- Quick photo-based updates.
- Drafts that can be completed later.
- Simple options to update or withdraw information when conditions change.

### 5. Warning Others and Finding Reporting Channels

He needs to warn others about hazards and know where to send reports.

**Possible solutions:**

- A directory of responsible organizations.
- Links to relevant official reporting forms.
- Reports containing a location and photo.
- Temporary community hazard alerts.
- Easily shareable warnings for friends and nearby users.
