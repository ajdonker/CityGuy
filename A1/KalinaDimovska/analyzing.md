## Why Was He/She Chosen? Why Is He/She Appropriate?

Kalina was chosen because she is a student currently living in Bologna who regularly uses public spaces such as parks, libraries, and study rooms. As an international student who does not speak Italian, she faces additional challenges regarding access to relevant local information. She previously resided in Skopje, where she has experienced difficulties in finding information about the local public infrastructure and ongoing events. In both places she likes to attend cultural events and likes to use online platforms and local recommendations to discover new places. As such, her needs as a user could be satisfied by a platform that addresses our previously agreed upon problem domain. 
## How Was He/She Recruited?

Kalina was recruited through personal contact with the interviewer. 

## Where Was the Interview Conducted?

The interview was conducted remotely through WhatsApp using a video call. The participant was located in Bologna. During the interview, there were minor technical difficulties with the video connection, but the conversation continued.

## What Did You Observe and Ask?

The interview focused on how Kalina discovers and uses public spaces, what information she needs before visiting them, and how she shares local knowledge with others.

She explained that she frequently visits parks for walking, exercising, and socializing, while libraries and study rooms are mainly used for studying. She also occasionally attends concerts, exhibitions, and public events.

When searching for unfamiliar places, she primarily uses Google Maps, asks friends, or checks Facebook and Reddit. However, she pointed out that information about accessibility, safety, events, and the current condition of public spaces is often incomplete or outdated.

She also discussed difficulties finding information about parking, accessibility for people with reduced mobility, temporary closures, and public transport disruptions. These difficulties are increased by the language barrier.

She is willing to share useful information and report public infrastructure problems to the relevant authorities. However, she feels discouraged by the lack of convenient reporting platforms, limited visibility of contributions, and absence of feedback from authorities.

Overall, the interview revealed a need for a centralized, accessible, and community-driven platform that provides reliable local information and supports meaningful citizen participation.

## Needs and possible solutions 

### 1. Reliable and Up-to-Date Information

Kalina needs current information about public spaces, their safety, condition, opening hours, and ongoing events.

**Possible solutions:**

- Regularly updated place information.
- Community-submitted updates and reviews.
- User verification of information.
- Notifications about changes and closures.

### 2. Accessibility and Transportation Information

She needs information about transportation options, parking availability, and accessibility for people with reduced mobility.

**Possible solutions:**

- Information about public transport, parking, and bicycle access.
- Accessibility details for entrances, stairs, and obstacles.
- Community reports about temporary accessibility issues.
- Accessibility filters when searching for places.

### 3. Centralized and Multilingual Local Information

She needs an easy way to discover local events, disruptions, and public service updates in a language she understands.

**Possible solutions:**
- A centralized feed of local news and events.
- Automatic translation of community information.
- Updates about road closures, strikes, and transport disruptions.
- Location-based notifications about nearby events and changes.

### 4. Easy Community Reporting and Feedback

Kalina wants to be able to report public space problems, share useful information, and receive feedback about her contribution.

**Possible solutions:**
- Quick issue reporting with photos and location.
- Community confirmation of reported problems.
- Forwarding reports to relevant public authorities.
- Status updates on reported issues.
- Notifications when contributions help others.
