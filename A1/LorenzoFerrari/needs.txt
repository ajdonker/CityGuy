### 1\. Evaluating Place Suitability in Advance (Environmental Conditions &amp; Amenities)

•⁠  ⁠*User Need Statement:*

&gt; "Lorenzo needs a way to *evaluate* environmental conditions (noise level, crowd density, shade) and amenity availability (water fountains, benches) before visiting a public space, so he can reliably find a quiet spot to relax without wasting time."

•⁠  ⁠*Evidence from Interview:* Lorenzo's main priorities when visiting a park are finding a quiet, isolated spot with shade, benches, and a water fountain to refill his bottle[3][4]. He noted that current tools like Google Maps fail to provide details about whether a park has water fountains, benches, or high noise levels[5].

---

### 2\. Discovering Unlisted Amenities &amp; Local Community Activities

•⁠  ⁠*User Need Statement:*

&gt; "Lorenzo needs a way to *discover* unlisted neighborhood amenities (such as new recycling machines) and informal community activities (such as running groups or board game clubs) that are poorly publicized on standard digital platforms."

•⁠  ⁠*Evidence from Interview:* Lorenzo discovered a new plastic bottle recycling machine near his house purely by walking past it, as it wasn't listed online[6]. He also expressed frustration that local activities like running groups or board game clubs are difficult to find online unless you search specifically or spot physical posters in the city[7].

---

### 3\. Reporting &amp; Tracking Urban Infrastructure Hazards

•⁠  ⁠*User Need Statement:*

&gt; "Lorenzo needs a way to *report* dangerous urban hazards to the responsible authorities and *track* repair progress, so he can ensure public safety and receive confirmation that his civic action made a difference."

•⁠  ⁠*Evidence from Interview:* Lorenzo reported a sharp, broken bench in front of the train station to station staff because it posed a physical danger to passersby[8]. Although the bench was fixed months later, he received no feedback or confirmation regarding his report[9].

---

### 4\. Selective Sharing &amp; Preserving Privacy for Quiet Spots

•⁠  ⁠*User Need Statement:*

&gt; "Lorenzo needs to be able to *control* how and with whom he shares information about quiet public places, so he can exchange helpful tips with close friends while keeping his personal relaxation sanctuaries private and uncrowded."

•⁠  ⁠*Evidence from Interview:* Lorenzo frequently exchanges practical city tips within his trusted circle of friends[10][11]. However, he deliberately avoids sharing his favorite secluded relaxation spots publicly because he fears that doing so would make them crowded and noisy[12].
