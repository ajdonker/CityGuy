Why was he chosen? Why is he appropriate?
Suggested rationale: Lorenzo regularly visits parks and squares in Cesena and discovers places through walking and recommendations from friends. His experiences are relevant to understanding how citizens find and share information about public spaces.

How was he recruited?
He's a friend of my friend's on Instagram, and I asked him to help us.

Where was the interview conducted?
At his home

What did you observe and ask?
He described visiting parks to read, walk, listen to music, and relax alone after frequent social interaction at work. He reported difficulty checking whether places have benches, water fountains, or a quiet environment. He exchanges updates with friends but keeps some quiet locations private because he fears they will become crowded. He also described reporting a broken bench to station staff and receiving no follow-up, although he later saw that it had been repaired. These findings come from his self-reported experiences during the interview.

Needs
He needs to find places where he can relax alone and meet his practical needs.
Possible solutions: Search by activity and facilities; recent visitor reports about noise and crowding; suggestions for quieter visiting times; community questions about specific places; maps showing benches, fountains, and shaded areas.

He needs to explore unfamiliar areas without losing his sense of direction.
Possible solutions: Downloadable offline maps; saved walking routes; directions using recognizable landmarks; a shortcut back to a familiar location; downloadable place information for use without connectivity.

He needs to preserve the quiet places he values while deciding what information to share.
Possible solutions: Private bookmarks for personal discoveries; sharing with selected friends; separate personal notes and public contributions; sharing facility updates at already well-known places; suggestions spread across several suitable locations to avoid concentrating visitors.

He needs to know whom to contact about damaged infrastructure and what happens afterward.
Possible solutions: A directory of responsible organizations; reports automatically routed to the appropriate contact; confirmation that a report was received; a page showing progress; notifications when repairs are completed.

He needs to discover local activities and groups that match his interests.
Possible solutions: A searchable local-group directory; a shared events calendar; updates posted by organizers; community contributions containing information from street posters; activity suggestions based on interests and availability.
