## Why Was He Chosen? Why Is He Appropriate?

He was our second-to-last interviewee. We had already interviewed five participants from East Asia and Europe, so we wanted to recruit someone from a different region. He was from Pakistan and had expressed dissatisfaction with Google Maps, so I selected him as a participant.

## How Was He Recruited?

I posted a message on a Chinese social media platform called REDnote, and he expressed willingness to help me.

## Where Was the Interview Conducted?

The interview was conducted via video call.

## What Did You Observe and Ask?

We asked Abuzar about the public spaces he visits, how he discovers unfamiliar places, and what information helps him decide whether a place is suitable. We also explored missing or inaccurate information, trust in citizen contributions, experiences of reporting problems, and motivations and barriers to sharing local knowledge.

He said that recent photos, detailed reviews, agreement between multiple contributors, and information from people he knows help him assess reliability. He described calling the police after a phone theft and subsequently sharing a warning with others. Although he shares information to help friends, family, and neighbours, hostile responses can make him reluctant to contribute publicly. These findings reflect his self-reported experiences during the video interview.

## Needs

### 1. Find Nearby Places That Suit His Activities

He needs to find nearby public spaces where he can walk, study, or meet friends in a suitably quiet environment.


### 2. Assess Safety Before Visiting

He needs to assess whether a place is safe and suitable for himself and the people using it, including women, children, and older people.


### 3. Obtain Current Information and Judge Its Reliability

He needs to obtain accurate, up-to-date information about a place and assess whether the information reflects its actual condition.

### 4. Understand Access Conditions and Available Facilities

He needs to find practical local information about how a public space can be used, including accessibility, facilities, transport, and special access times.

### 5. Know How to Report Problems and Seek Help

He needs to know whom to contact when he encounters damaged facilities, safety incidents, or a need for urgent assistance in a public space.

### 6. Share Useful Information Without Hostile Responses

He needs to share useful local information without being discouraged by hostile or dismissive reactions from other users.
